2-Top — privacy

2-Top has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page says everything the game stores or sends, and when.

On your device

A random install id, the name you dial, a signing key made on the device, your settings, your record against each opponent, and your match tapes. None of it leaves the device unless something below sends it.

When you play online

The game asks a matchmaking server to pair it with another player, and then talks to that player's device directly (WebRTC). Like any direct connection, it reveals your IP address to the other player. When a direct connection is impossible, traffic passes through a relay (TURN), which forwards it without storing it. Your install id, name and public signing key go to your opponent, so each of you can keep a record of the other and sign results together.

Only when you choose to share

  • SHARE uploads that match's tape to the tape relay so the link works. Tapes expire after about a week.
  • GO LIVE streams your next duel's inputs to the relay while it is played, for anyone with the link. Your opponent's screen shows LIVE.
  • SEND MY SHADE puts your name, install id and four play-style numbers into a link.
  • LADDER ON publishes your signed results, including names, scores and public keys, on the ladder page. It does this only when your opponent has the ladder on too.

Crashes

A crash log stays on the device. You decide whether to send it to anyone.

Removing it

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the device. Shared tapes expire from the relay by themselves. To have a ladder entry or a shared tape removed sooner, open an issue on the project's repository.
